About

My Journey

I’m a senior technical writer with an M.A. in Applied Linguistics and a B.A. in English Literature, a background that shapes how I think about clarity, structure and the reader.

I started in 2019 at HighRadius and have since written for fintech, automotive software and enterprise planning products. Over the last few years at Planful, I moved from product documentation into UX content, video, API docs and AI-driven content workflows.

I like work where words, design and data meet, and where a good explanation measurably helps users.
